Method and coverage

Evaluation follows the MPP definition in Evaluating the Rationales of Amateur Investors.

Maximum Possible Profit

A post dated t enters at the adjusted opening price of the first trading day after the post. The horizon endpoint T moves forward whenever the evaluator is rerun.

Bullish MPP = (max High[t+1..T] - Open[t+1]) / Open[t+1] Bearish MPP = (Open[t+1] - min Low[t+1..T]) / Open[t+1]

OHLC values are back-adjusted across official ex-right and ex-dividend events. TPEx Emerging Stock Board positions use the daily negotiated average as an explicit entry proxy because that market has no official opening price. Pairwise ties receive 0.5 accuracy.

Portfolio interpretation

The Top-210 score is the equal-weight mean of constituent MPP values. It measures the opportunity captured by the selected opinions, not a simultaneously executable realized return.

  • Each team keeps exactly 210 allocation slots.
  • The baseline keeps exactly 2,100 allocation slots.
  • Posts lacking an auditable symbol, stance, or price stay in cash at 0%.
  • Coverage is shown beside every portfolio result.
