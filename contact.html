Why Join

What You Get at HAA Lab

A Track Record That Opens Doors

Research from the lab and our collaborators has earned the ANLP Outstanding Paper Award (top 2%), the Hon Hai Technology Award, TSC Thesis Awards, and national scholarships, with work appearing at top venues such as ACL, EMNLP, SIGIR, and WWW.

A Truly International Lab

English is our working language. Members and collaborators span Japan, Taiwan, Germany, the UK, India, Korea, France, Thailand, and so on — and the network keeps growing every year.

Mentorship With Community Access

Your PI founded ACL SIG-FinTech, co-chairs NTCIR-19, and serves as Senior Area Chair at ACL and EMNLP — you'll be introduced to the community from day one, not after graduation.

Research That Matters

We work on high-stakes problems — financial decisions, misinformation, regulatory compliance, and biomedical applications — with real datasets, industry collaborations, awards, and national media coverage.

The HAA Moment

We chase the "はぁ〜" — that sigh of relief and satisfaction when human-agent collaboration truly works. Research here is about creating moments people are grateful for.

Tokyo, at the Heart of It

Based at the National Institute of Informatics in central Tokyo — one of Asia's leading informatics institutes, steps from Jimbocho and the Imperial Palace.
