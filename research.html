What does a human-agent society need?

Our research spans the full stack of that question — from how agents are built, to how they team up with people in consequential decisions, to how societies adapt when agents become everyday allies.

Pillar 01

Agent Design & Agentic Frameworks

Information Retrieval for Agents

How IR techniques enhance agent capabilities, focusing on retrieval-augmented generation and knowledge grounding.

LLM-based Agent Architectures

Designing and optimizing agentic frameworks powered by large language models for improved reasoning and task execution.

Performance & Efficiency

Benchmarking and enhancing IR-LLM integrated systems in real-world agent applications.

Pillar 02

Human-Agent Teaming

Higher-Order Thinking Augmentation

Enhancing human cognitive capabilities through agent collaboration — complex problem-solving and creative thinking.

High-Stakes Decision-Making

Frameworks for human-agent collaboration in critical scenarios where decisions have significant consequences.

High-Fidelity Interaction

Seamless, intuitive interfaces that enable natural and effective communication between humans and agents.

Pillar 03

Human-Agent Society

Societal Transformation

How the integration of agents reshapes social structures, norms, and relationships.

Impact Analysis

The economic, cultural, and ethical implications of widespread agent adoption in everyday life.

Governance & Policy Design

Governance, policy, and regulatory approaches for responsible agent deployment at societal scale.

Our Approach

From Benchmarks to Real-World Impact

We ground our research in high-stakes domains — finance, law, policy, and scholarly communication — where getting human-agent collaboration right matters most.

Build Benchmarks

We create the datasets and shared tasks the community evaluates against — FinNum, FinArg, NumEval, PromiseEval, RegCom, and more at NTCIR and SemEval.

Publish at Top Venues

Our work appears at ACL, EMNLP, SIGIR, WWW, and NAACL — spanning NLP, IR, and web research communities.

Partner with Industry

Joint projects on business ideation, live commentary generation, and human-centered AI with industrial partners.

Interested in Collaboration?

We're always looking for passionate researchers and partners who share our vision of creating a better human-agent society.
