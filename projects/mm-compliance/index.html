Why MM-Compliance

Compliance evidence does not live in one language—or one modality.

MM-Compliance aligns industry-specific SASB metrics with real disclosure pages from corporate ESG and sustainability reports. Its completeness annotations distinguish fully compliant evidence from partial or superficial mentions.

01 / LANGUAGE 6

Languages

English, French, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, and Thai—covering linguistic and regional reporting variation.

02 / DOCUMENTS 7,174

PDF pages

Original page layouts preserve text, tables, charts, infographics, and other visual structures important to ESG auditing.

03 / FRAMEWORK 366

SASB metric types

Page-level evidence is aligned with industry-specific metrics and labeled for complete versus incomplete disclosure.

Evaluation benchmark

From finding the page to verifying the disclosure.

The benchmark separates two stages of a practical auditing workflow: locating relevant evidence in a long report, then deciding whether the evidence fully satisfies a reporting requirement.

Task 01

Page-level Compliance Judgment

Given a SASB metric and one report page, predict yes, yes but not complete, or no.

Task 02

Metric-to-Page Evidence Retrieval

Given a metric description and a multi-page ESG report, rank the pages that contain the supporting evidence.

Key finding

Current models can often shortlist relevant evidence pages—reaching an average Hit@20 of 0.882—but still struggle to distinguish partial disclosures from complete compliance. Requirement-aware completeness verification remains the central bottleneck.
